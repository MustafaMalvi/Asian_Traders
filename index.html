Our Story

Three decades of excellence

Established in 1990, Asian Traders has been the cornerstone of hardware supply in Bhavnagar for over three decades. Founded with a vision to bring quality and reliability to every home and business, we have grown to become one of the most trusted names in the region.

Our store, nestled in the bustling Vora Bazar, carries an extensive range of products — from premium furniture handles and kitchen baskets to robust door hardware and bathroom accessories. Every item in our catalog is carefully selected to meet our standards of quality and durability.

At Asian Traders, we believe good hardware is the foundation of beautiful spaces. Our experienced team is always on hand to guide you to the right product for your project — whether it's a single cabinet renovation or an entire home fit-out.
